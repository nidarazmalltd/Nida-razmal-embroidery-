About Us

Nida Razmal LTD Embroidery Company is a professional embroidery and textile company dedicated to providing high-quality embroidery products and services. We focus on quality, precision, modern designs, and customer satisfaction.

Our company works with computerized embroidery technology and offers customized embroidery solutions for businesses, brands, garments, uniforms, textiles, and other products.

We are committed to delivering reliable quality, competitive prices, and professional service to our customers. Whether you need customized embroidery, embroidery thread, textile products, or large-scale production, our team is ready to support your requirements.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality embroidery and textile solutions while building long-term relationships with customers through trust, quality, and professional service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted embroidery and textile company in Afghanistan and expand our products and services to international markets.
